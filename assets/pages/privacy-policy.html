This policy explains what information GroupChatOn Tools (infinity.groupchaton.com) collects when you use it, how it is used and the choices you have. It is part of GroupChatOn (www.groupchaton.com).

Your files stay on your device

Every tool here works inside your own browser. The PDFs, images, text and files you choose are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Close the page and they are gone.

A few tools need an outside service to do their job:

  • URL Shortener sends the long link you enter to v.gd (or is.gd as a backup), which creates the short link.
  • Speech to Text uses your browser’s own speech recognition. In some browsers, such as Chrome, the browser sends your voice to its maker’s speech service to turn it into text.
  • Image to Text (OCR) downloads its reading engine and language data from a public library server (jsDelivr). Your image is not sent.
  • PDF, image and ZIP tools download their open-source libraries from public library servers (cdnjs). Your files are not sent.
  • WhatsApp Direct Message opens WhatsApp with the number and message you typed.

Information you give us

When you use Ask for a Tool, you send us your name (optional), your email address and your request. We use them only to reply and to plan new tools, and we never sell or share them.

Information collected automatically

  • Google Analytics counts visits and the tools used (approximate location, device and browser) so we know which tools help most. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.
  • Google AdSense shows ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info. Read how Google uses data from partner sites.
  • Blogger (Google) hosts this site and keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Stored on your own device

Some tools remember your last choices (for example the language or the text in the word counter), and the request form can remember your name and email. This stays in your browser’s local storage, is never sent to us, and is removed when you clear your browser data.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. You can block or delete them in your browser settings; the tools still work, but ads may become less relevant.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, through Ask for a Tool or the GroupChatOn contact page. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, and we will honour them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
