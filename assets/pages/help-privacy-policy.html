This policy explains what information GroupChatOn Help (help.groupchaton.com) collects when you read its guides, contact support or leave a comment, how it is used and the choices you have. GroupChatOn Help is part of GroupChatOn (www.groupchaton.com) and Infinity by GroupChatOn (infinity.groupchaton.com).

Reading the help articles

You can read every guide without an account and without giving us any personal information. The guides explain how to use the free Infinity tools, how to read novels on GroupChatOn, and how to fix common problems.

Information you give us

  • Contact support: when you use the Contact support form, you send us your name (optional), your email address, the topic and your message. The form is sent through Blogger’s own contact form, so it arrives in our email inbox. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem, and we never sell or share it.
  • Comments: comments on articles use Disqus. Disqus loads only when you tap the button to show comments. If you post a comment, Disqus handles your account, name and comment under its own privacy policy. Comments you post are public.

Information collected automatically

  • Google Analytics counts visits and which articles are read (approximate location, device and browser), so we know which guides help most and which need work. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.
  • Google AdSense shows ads that keep these guides free. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info. Read how Google uses data from partner sites.
  • Blogger (Google) hosts this site and keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Stored on your own device

The site remembers a few choices in your browser, such as light or dark mode, and the support form can remember your name and email so you do not have to type them again. This stays in your browser’s local storage, is never sent to us, and is removed when you clear your browser data.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Google Analytics, Google AdSense and Disqus (once you open comments) may set them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the guides still work, but ads may become less relevant.

The free tools

When an article links to a tool on Infinity, that tool runs in your own browser and your files are not uploaded. The Infinity Privacy Policy explains the few tools that use an outside service.

How long we keep messages

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to solve your problem and follow up, and are then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, through Contact support. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California or the DPDP Act in India), you may have further rights, and we will honour them.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Also see our Disclaimer and the Terms of Use.
